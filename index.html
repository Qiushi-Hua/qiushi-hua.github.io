What I do

Causal inference on macro shocks

I use local projections and panel methods to trace how a monetary policy shock moves firm outcomes over the quarters that follow, and to separate effects on quantity from effects on quality.

Firm-level data at scale

I join Compustat, USPTO, CRSP and FRED into reproducible panels, reconcile inconsistent entity IDs, and keep a change log so results can be rebuilt from raw files.

Structural models

I solve heterogeneous-firm dynamic programs by value function iteration in Julia and Matlab, check numerical stability with forward and backward passes, then simulate transition paths after an aggregate shock.

Text and machine learning

I built an NLP pipeline over millions of online job postings that classifies roles, extracts skills, and tracks employer demand by industry and region, reported through interactive dashboards.
