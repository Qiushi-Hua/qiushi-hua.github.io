Methods and data

Most of my work is empirical. I line a series of monetary policy shocks up against firm-level outcomes and estimate the response over the following quarters with local projections. Building the panel is usually the longer half of the job. For the job market paper I had to match patents to the firms that filed them and reconcile identifiers that disagree across Compustat, USPTO and CRSP.

When a question needs structure rather than a regression I solve heterogeneous-firm models numerically, in Julia and Matlab, by value function iteration.

One project sits well outside macro. I built a pipeline that reads several million online job postings and tracks which skills employers ask for, by industry and region. Topic modelling and a supervised classifier do the text work. The output is a dashboard.
