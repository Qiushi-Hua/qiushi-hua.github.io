Monetary Economics  ·  University of Florida

I am a PhD candidate in economics at the University of Florida and I finish in April 2027. My job market paper asks what a monetary policy shock does to corporate innovation. Building the panel out of Compustat, USPTO and CRSP took longer than the estimation did, since patent assignees and firm identifiers do not line up cleanly. I also work on firm investment under adjustment costs, which I solve numerically, and on one project outside macro that reads several million job postings to see which skills employers are asking for.

I am looking for economist or data scientist positions and can relocate. I work in Python, Julia, R, Stata and SQL.
